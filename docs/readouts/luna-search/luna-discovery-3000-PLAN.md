  --checkpoint /out/checkpoint.json \
  --generation 3000 --generations 3001 \
  --count 12 --games 240 --total-games 240 \

Therefore the expected 5,760 raw discovery games are twelve candidates × two gates × 240 games, with AB2 seeds `310000000..310000239` and shipped seeds `310050000..310050239` for each candidate. There is no confirmation stage because `games == total-games`; any promotion/offspring metadata is ignored for inference, and the raw discovery records are the primary output.

The isolated checkpoint must be initialized in this root, and the original saved checkpoint and earlier raw records are outside the mount. Structural-family experiments reserve seed families at 400,000,000 and above; this run's 310m families are disjoint.

No strength claim or candidate selection is made from this experiment alone.
